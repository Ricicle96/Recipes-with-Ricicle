Useful Tools

A collection of kithcen tools I've found genuinely useful over the years. These are the kinds of items that make cooking easier, more consistent, and a lot more enjoyable.

Some links on this page will take you to external retailers where the item can be purchased.

Chef's Knife

A good chef’s knife is one of the most important tools in any kitchen. It makes prep faster, safer, and much more enjoyable. You do not need the most expensive option, just something comfortable in the hand with a decent edge.

View on ProCook

Digital Kitchen Scales

Digital scales help with accuracy and consistency, especially for baking, meal prep, and portioning ingredients. Measuring by weight is often far more reliable than using cups or rough estimates.

View on ProCook

Microplane Grater

Ideal for garlic, parmesan, citrus zest, nutmeg and ginger. It is one of those tools that seems small and simple, but makes a huge difference in both flavour and texture.

View on ProCook

Instant Read Thermometer

Great for taking the guesswork out of cooking meat, poultry and even baked items. It helps you avoid overcooking while also making sure food is properly cooked through.
