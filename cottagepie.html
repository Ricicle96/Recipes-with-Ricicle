Cottage Pie

Cottage Pie is a classic British comfort dish made with rich, savoury minced beef cooked in a hearty gravy with onions, carrots and herbs, topped with a layer of fluffy mashed potatoes and baked until golden. Warming, rustic and deeply satisfying, it’s the perfect one-dish meal for cosy evenings.

Traditionally made as a way to use up leftover roast beef, Cottage Pie has long been a staple of British home cooking. The beef filling is slowly simmered to develop depth and richness, often enhanced with Worcestershire sauce, tomato purée and a splash of stock, before being topped with creamy mashed potatoes that crisp beautifully in the oven.

Prep Time 15 minutes
Cook Time 1 hour
Difficulty Easy
Serves 4

Method

Beef base

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan / gas mark 6).
  2. On the hob, heat the oil in a large, deep frying pan. Add the onion and carrot and cook gently until softened - around 3 - 4 minutes.
  3. Add the crushed garlic and cook for a further 2 minutes.
  4. Add the beef, stirring well, until the meat is broken up and browned all over.
  5. Add the flour, a little bit at a time, and stir throughly. When all the flour is incorporated, add the beef stock, tomato purée, and bring to the boil. Reduce the heat to a simmer.
  6. Add the Worcestershire sauce, red wine, peas. Season with salt and pepper, cover the pan, and cook for around 15 minutes. The juices should thicken up during this process.

Mashed Potatoes

  1. Whilst you're cooking the beef base, cook the potatoes in a large pan of boiling salted water until soft, which will take around 20 minutes.
  2. Drain the potatoes and pass them through a potato ricer into a large bowl.
  3. Add the butter and mash with your masher.
  4. Once the butter is combined, add the milk, stirring it to combine and season with salt and pepper to taste.

Assemble and Bake

  1. Transfer the beef base into a large, ovenproof dish.
  2. Cover with the mashed potatoes.
  3. Drag a fork over the top of the mashed potatoes to create a decorative pattern.
  4. Cook in the oven for 25-30 minutes, until the top is golden brown and bubbling.
  5. Sprinkle the grated cheddar cheese over the top of the mashed potatoes.
  6. Return to the oven for a further 5-10 minutes, until the cheese is melted and golden.
