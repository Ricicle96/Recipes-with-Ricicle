Tips and Tricks

A collection of simple, practical tips that make cooking easier, tastier, and a lot less stressful — whether you’re just starting out or you’re trying to level up your basics.

Prep before you cook

Chop, measure and get everything ready before the hob goes on. It keeps you calm, prevents burning, and makes cooking feel far less chaotic — especially for sauces and stir-frys.

Season as you go

Don’t wait until the end to add salt. Add small amounts throughout cooking to build flavour gradually — you’ll need less overall, and everything tastes more balanced.

Taste constantly (and adjust)

Recipes are a guide, not the law. Taste sauces, gravies and fillings as you go and adjust: salt for flavour, lemon/vinegar for brightness, and a pinch of sugar if something is too sharp.

Control your heat

Most home cooking is better on medium than blasting high. Medium heat gives you browning without burning and lets flavours develop properly — especially with onions, garlic and spices.

Let meat rest

After cooking, rest meat for 5–10 minutes before slicing. It helps the juices redistribute so it stays tender instead of running dry all over the board.

Don’t overcrowd the pan

If the pan is packed, ingredients steam instead of brown. Cook in batches when you need good colour on mince, chicken, mushrooms, or roasted veg.

Use the right pan size

Too small and everything steams. Too big and sauces reduce too quickly. Matching pan size to the amount you’re cooking makes results more consistent.

Learn a few “rescue” moves

Too salty? Add a splash of water/stock or extra veg. Too bland? Salt, acid (lemon/vinegar), or a bit of butter. Too thin? Simmer uncovered. Too thick? Loosen with stock.

Keep your knife sharp

A sharp knife is safer and faster. A blunt knife slips. If you only do one “tool” habit, make it keeping your main knife sharp (or using a sharpener now and then).

Read the recipe once before starting

It sounds obvious, but it prevents surprises — like needing to preheat the oven, soften butter, or reduce a sauce for 15 minutes at the end.
